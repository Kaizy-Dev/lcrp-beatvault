Last updated: August 27, 2026

1. Information We Store

LCRP BeatVault may store information required for its features, including Discord server IDs, user IDs, server configuration, economy balances, playlists, shop items, inventory data, and TTS configuration.

2. Why We Store This Information

This information is used only to provide and maintain bot features such as server configuration, music playlists, economy systems, shop functionality, and text-to-speech settings.

3. Messages

When TTS is enabled, messages may be processed in order to provide the text-to-speech functionality. The bot does not need to store message content permanently to provide this feature.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Some bot features may require sending limited information to third-party services necessary to provide those features.

5. Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect stored bot data. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Removal

Server administrators may request deletion of stored server data. Requests should be directed to the bot developer.

7. Children's Privacy

LCRP BeatVault is provided through Discord and is subject to Discord's own age requirements and policies.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the bot's functionality or data practices change.

9. Contact

For privacy-related questions or data removal requests, contact the bot developer: kaizy_dude.
